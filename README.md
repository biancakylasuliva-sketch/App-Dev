# Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021)

## A. Introduction

### Movie Overview

**Spider-Man: No Way Home** is a superhero movie released in 2021, directed by Jon Watts and starring Tom Holland. The movie follows Peter Parker after his identity as Spider-Man is revealed to the world. He asks Doctor Strange for help, but a mistake causes villains from different universes to enter his world.

- **Genre:** Action, Adventure, Science Fiction, Superhero
- **Release Year:** 2021
- **Director:** Jon Watts
- **Main Actor:** Tom Holland
- **Duration:** 2 hours and 28 minutes

---

## B. Synopsis

The story begins when Peter Parker's identity as Spider-Man becomes public. This causes problems for him and the people close to him. He asks Doctor Strange to cast a spell that will make everyone forget his identity, but the spell goes wrong.

As a result, villains from different universes enter his world, including Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, Electro, Sandman, and the Lizard. Peter decides to help these villains instead of simply sending them back to their universes. During his journey, he meets two other versions of Spider-Man played by Tobey Maguire and Andrew Garfield.

Together, the three Spider-Men fight the villains and try to save everyone. However, Peter experiences a heartbreaking loss and must make a difficult decision. In the end, he sacrifices his personal happiness to protect the people he loves and the entire world.

> "With great power comes great responsibility."

This famous lesson reminds us that having abilities and power also means having the responsibility to use them wisely.

## C. Characters

Here are the important characters in the movie:

1. **Peter Parker / Spider-Man (Tom Holland)** – A young superhero who protects people and learns the importance of sacrifice and responsibility.
2. **MJ (Zendaya)** – Peter's girlfriend who supports him during difficult situations.
3. **Ned Leeds (Jacob Batalon)** – Peter's best friend who helps him throughout his journey.
4. **Doctor Strange (Benedict Cumberbatch)** – A powerful sorcerer who tries to help Peter fix the problems caused by the spell.
5. **Green Goblin (Willem Dafoe)** – One of the main villains who causes serious problems for Peter.
6. **Spider-Man (Andrew Garfield)** – Another version of Peter Parker who comes from a different universe.
7. **Spider-Man (Tobey Maguire)** – An older and more experienced Spider-Man who guides the younger Peter.
